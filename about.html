About Us

We’re building a simpler way to connect people with the right help

All Rounder is designed to solve a common everyday problem: finding trusted local help quickly and making it easy for skilled taskers to reach people who need them.

“We started with a simple goal — to make it easier for users to find reliable taskers and for taskers to discover meaningful local work.”

Whether someone needs a quick service, a trusted professional, or a better way to get work done, the idea is to reduce delays, build trust, and keep the process simple.

Our Mission & Vision

Building a Better Future for Local Work

▶
👥

Our Mission

To make local hiring easier and more trustworthy by helping people quickly connect with the right taskers for everyday needs.

We want to remove friction from the process and make it easier for service providers to discover real opportunities nearby.

👁️

Our Vision

To create a more connected local marketplace where users can find help faster and taskers can access real work with confidence.

We aim to make local services more accessible, transparent, and practical for everyday life.

Quick Matching Connect users with nearby taskers faster.
Trust & Clarity Help people choose the right professional with confidence.
Local Growth Create better opportunities for workers and users alike.
